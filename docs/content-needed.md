these as real material becomes available (props only — layout is ready).
Nothing below may be invented; each entry needs a real source.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Copy & facts

- Short bio (About page — beyond the approved brand statement)
- Publication list (titles, venues, years — for Insights)
- CoSlide description + hero screenshot/demo link
- Email address and ORCID iD (footer links)
- External profiles worth linking (beyond GitHub)

## Rules

- No stock photography, AI-generated research imagery, or borrowed figures.
- No unverifiable claims (no "leading expert" style copy, ever).
